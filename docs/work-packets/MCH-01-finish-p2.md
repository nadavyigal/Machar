## Task

### Story 1: make the fidelity check a 20-minute human task
The check is currently unbounded reading, which is why it has not happened. Add `scripts/printSchoolYear.mjs` that prints every closure and short day from `src/lib/calendar/data/2026-2027.ts` as a sorted, dated, Hebrew-named checklist with the day of the week for each date, ready to compare line by line against הודעה מס' 0363.

Include in the output the four caveats already recorded in the data file header: the middle/high school end date, the two year typos in the circular's own text, the unnamed Yom Kippur to Sukkot bridge closure, and the uncertain gan treatment of יום הזיכרון.

Write the result to `docs/fidelity-check-2026-2027.md` with an unticked checkbox per row. Do not tick any box: the founder ticks them.

### Story 2: edit and delete children after onboarding
Add a settings route reachable from the calendar screen that lists the household's children and allows renaming, changing level or week pattern, deleting, and adding another. `HouseholdStore` already has `add`, `remove(atOffsets:)` and `replaceAll`; add an `update` and keep persistence atomic.

Files: create `ios/Machar/Views/HouseholdSettingsView.swift`; modify `ios/Machar/Model/HouseholdStore.swift`, `ios/Machar/Views/HouseholdCalendarView.swift`.

### Story 3: day detail
Tapping a day in the month grid opens a sheet showing each child by name with their status and the Hebrew reason. Reuse `HouseholdDay.perChild`; no new calendar logic.

Files: create `ios/Machar/Views/DayDetailView.swift`; modify `ios/Machar/Views/HouseholdCalendarView.swift`.

### Story 4: get it on the phone (human step)
Run on the founder's own iPhone from Xcode with a free provisioning profile. Record in `tasks/progress.md` the date it was installed and the iOS version. Do not create an App Store record, a TestFlight build, or a distribution certificate.
